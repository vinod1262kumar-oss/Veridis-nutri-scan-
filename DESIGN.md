## Brand & Style

The design system projects clinical precision, metabolic intelligence, and dark luxury. It treats nutritional data not as domestic kitchen labels, but as high-fidelity biological diagnostics. Designed for discerning bio-hackers, performance athletes, and health-conscious professionals, the interface evokes the authoritative confidence of high-end aerospace diagnostics fused with laboratory-grade purity.

Visual tone centers on **Precision Dark Tech Glassmorphism**:
- Obsidian and deep charcoal subterranean surfaces eliminate ocular fatigue while creating a pure void for hyper-saturated luminescent indicators to signal metabolic quality.
- Micro-fine hairline borders (1px with sub-10% opacity) delineate structural matrices without heavy visual mass.
- Laser-focused data HUDs, glowing radial telemetry rings, and scan reticles establish a tactical, diagnostic feel that elevates every ingredient analysis to an executive biological readout.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is constructed upon pure negative space punctuated by high-luminescence status signals:

- **Primary (`#00F2A5` - Luminescent Emerald):** Denotes optimal clean-label grades, zero-additive purity, and validated organic status. Carries a soft cyan undertone to prevent organic cliché and evoke biomedical advancement.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Optical Ice Cyan):** Reserved for HUD reticles, optical scanning targeting frames, camera viewport highlights, and technical system telemetry.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Electric Amber):** Deployed for moderate alerts, ambiguous additives, elevated glycemic loads, and daily allowance thresholds.
- **Danger Alert (`#EF4444` - Pulse Crimson):** Strictly isolates ultra-processed indicators, harmful endocrine disruptors, allergens, and toxic chemical flags.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `Canvas / Void`: `#090D10`
  - `Card / Base Surface`: `#0E141B`
  - `Elevated Surface / Overlays`: `#151D24`
  - `Interactive Hover`: `#1E2832`
- **Contrast / Borders:** Hairline separation utilizes `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` for dormant panels, shifting to `rgba(0, 242, 165, 0.35)` or `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` under focus, scan, or active selection.

## Typography

Typography establishes an unambiguous partition between human-centric narrative and laboratory telemetry:

1. **Display & Editorial (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** High-clarity geometric architecture engineered with tight tracking for clean impact across main diagnostic titles, food product designations, and executive conclusions.
2. **Analysis & Narrative (`Inter`):** Neutral, utilitarian structure that ensures effortless scan-legibility across dense macro breakdowns, scientific ingredient profiles, and allergen contraindications.
3. **Telemetry & Calibration (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied across all quantitative metrics—biological ratings, E-number codes, barcode numbers, exact milligram measurements, HUD coordinate tracking, and nutritional variance deltas. Always displayed in tabular figure formats to guarantee strict vertical column alignment across comparison matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured on a 12-column dynamic fluid grid on desktop (1440px max wrapper), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (768px - 1024px) and a unified single/double-stack column on mobile screens (<768px).

- **Grid Mechanics:** Gutters are calibrated to `1.5rem` (24px) on desktop to provide spatial insulation between high-density data modules, condensing to `0.75rem` (12px) on viewport constraints to maximize scan area.
- **Component Breathing Rhythm:**
  - Micro-spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) handles internal badge padding, metric-to-unit lockups, and micro barcode indicators.
  - Core pacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`) establishes relationships within telemetry cards, card section splits, and camera targeting guides.
  - Layout breaks (`space-xl`) cleanly delineate major platform sections: Scan Viewport, Comparative Analysis Grid, and Ingredient Hazard Breakdown.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical dark glass and spectral back-lighting rather than traditional drop shadows. Traditional dark shadows dissolve into the deep obsidian canvas; spatial hierarchy is governed by light penetration, blur diffusion, and luminous glow radii:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#090D10`.
- **Level 1 (Telemetry Panels & Default Cards):** Semi-translucent `#0E141B` with `88%` alpha, `16px` backdrop-filter blur, and a uniform `1px` perimeter border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Inspect Panels / Active Diagnostics):** Layered `#151D24` at `92%` alpha, `24px` backdrop blur, `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, and a deep ambient glow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Level 3 (Focused HUD & Warning Alerts):**
  - **Clean Status (Optimal):** Glow border `rgba(0, 242, 165, 0.4)` paired with an inner and outer luminescent halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 242, 165, 0.25)`.
  - **Biohazard / Ultra-Processed Warning:** Glow border `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.45)` accompanied by a crimson diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The design system operates with **Soft (`1`)** geometry (0.25rem / 4px base radius, 0.5rem / 8px for standard containers, and 0.75rem / 12px for hero modules). 

This restrained curvature creates an uncompromising, technical aesthetic evocative of military optics and precision medical monitors, deliberately rejecting consumer-grade bubbly pill curves. Fully circular geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) is reserved solely for circular dial meters, radial biometric health scores, and targeted point reticles.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Cyber-Action (Trigger Scan / Analyze):** Solid `#00F2A5` background, obsidian text (`#090D10`), semi-bold typography, 8px radius. Hover triggers an outer neon diffusion aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(0, 242, 165, 0.4)`) with micro-scale upscaling (1.01).
- **Secondary Reticle Button:** Translucent `#0E141B` with `1px` border in `#06B6D4`, text in `#06B6D4`. Hover fills with `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`.
- **Destructive / Override:** Hairline Crimson `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)` border with muted red font, activating high-intensity red illumination on hover.

### 2. Optical Scanning Reticle & Frame
- Viewport features four 90-degree corner brackets in `#06B6D4` with an animated horizontal laser sweep line (`#00F2A5` with linear gradient fade and box-shadow glow).
- Real-time bounding boxes around detected barcodes and nutrition labels use monospaced data tags anchored to the top-right corner.

### 3. Radial Health Score Telemetry Meter
- Dual concentric SVG rings on a dark slate track (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- The active sweep path uses a dynamic conical gradient terminating in an acute glowing cap indicator.
- Center displays the numeric rating (0–100) rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`data-display-lg`), accompanied by a micro label denoting classification (`OPTIMAL`, `CAUTION`, `CRITICAL`).

### 4. Hazard & Additive Badges
- Compact rectangular chips with 4px corner radii, structured as `[STATUS ICON] [E-NUMBER / CHEMICAL] [RISK GRADE]`.
- Background set to `8%` alpha tint of the risk color with a `1px` border at `25%` opacity (e.g., Red 40 Dye = Crimson tint + Crimson border).

### 5. Macro Breakdown Bars
- Segmented multi-chromatic horizontal track with 2px gaps between macro categories (Proteins, Lipids, Carbohydrates, Dietary Fiber).
- Hovering reveals precise milligram tooltips styled as dark floating glass HUD panels.

### 6. Side-by-Side Matrix Comparison
- Paired columnar cards with synchronized scrolling. Conflicting nutrients highlight differentials with directional indicators (`+14% Sugar`, `-42% Sodium`) rendered in JetBrains Mono using respective status colors.
