How to read these results

VERIDIS can explain label terms and point out items worth checking, but a label alone cannot establish a person's biological response, prove an ingredient causes disease, or measure contaminants. An AI-generated explanation may be wrong; verify important claims with reliable sources and follow the product label. If you have an allergy, use the package's allergen statement and consult a qualified professional when needed.
