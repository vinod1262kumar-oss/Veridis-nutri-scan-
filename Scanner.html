Understand the label

What can we learn?

A food package can provide useful information, but it does not tell the whole story.

Information this app can examine

  • Visible ingredients and nutrition values.
  • Serving sizes and declared nutrient amounts.
  • Possible meanings of unfamiliar ingredients.
  • Whether package claims match the supplied label.

What it cannot verify from a photo

  • Unlisted ingredients or missing nutrient values.
  • Laboratory purity or contamination.
  • Hyperspectral or molecular measurements.
